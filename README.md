# Another Life

An anime isekai life RPG for your phone. Make a character, get reborn, and live a whole life:
childhood in Mirebrook, the Royal Academy, then adulthood as an adventurer, café owner or knight,
while the Demon Lord stirs in the north.

**Play:** https://lucentvd.github.io/another-life/

Add it to your home screen (Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen, Chrome: ⋮ → Install app) and it
runs full screen and offline. Saves stay on your device.
